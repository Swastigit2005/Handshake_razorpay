Revenue recovery for AI shoppers

You can’t email a bot a coupon.

AI agents now buy things for people. When one gives up at checkout, it leaves no email and no cart to remind. Handshake reads the store’s own traffic, works out why the agent left, and sends it an offer it can act on, within rules the merchant controls.

13 + open

known causes (a missing spec, a price jump, a spending cap, an OTP wall…), plus problems outside that list, which it names from the traffic and hands to a person.

11 rules

decide whether Handshake may act at all: re-offer limits, a margin floor, quiet hours, a confidence threshold and a kill switch. No model can override them.

How it knows why

  • Payment-rail codes are trusted most, then what the agent did (which product it opened, what the quote said), then what the agent claims. A claim can confirm the evidence, never replace it.
  • When the evidence is thin, it says “unknown” and sends the case to a person rather than guess.
  • A problem outside the known list is named, but never acted on automatically. A person approves the offer first.

What this is not, yet

  • A sandbox: no real money moves. Payments are simulated; Shopify orders run in catalogue mode until Shopify approves order access.
  • Tested on a handful of real-agent runs and hundreds of scripted ones, not on production traffic at volume.
  • Built as a portfolio project, end to end: SDK, API, PostgreSQL, signed webhooks, MCP, a Shopify connector.
