Why did the AI shopper stop?

A person who abandons a cart leaves no reason. An AI agent doesn't say why either. But it leaves something a person never does: a complete record of every request it made and every answer it got. Send a shopper you know nothing about into a shop with problems you can't see. Handshake has to work out, from the shop's own server log, why it stopped, and what to offer it so it comes back and pays.

QUESTION 1 · WHY DID IT STOP?

Evidence, not a menu

Handshake reads only what the shop's server saw, plus the shop's catalogue. It asks three kinds of question:

  • Known signatures: 13 causes with exact fingerprints, like a 402 insufficient_funds or a spec missing from the record served
  • General questions: did the shop refuse the agent's last request? Was what the agent saw the worst of its options on some dimension? These need no rule written in advance.
  • An analyst model (when a key is configured) for traces nothing else explains. It must cite the events, and it may name a cause nobody listed.
a cause, a confidence and a drafted fix
QUESTION 2 · SHOULD WE ACT?

11 fixed rules, then a person

The rules don't diagnose anything. They decide whether acting is allowed.

  • A confident, known cause with a safe fix: Handshake acts on its own, inside the limits
  • Anything outside the playbook, or not confident enough: Handshake drafts the re-offer, and a person approves it
  • A model can explain and draft; it can never move money
