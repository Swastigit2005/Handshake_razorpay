Why did the AI shopper stop?

A person who abandons a cart leaves no reason. An AI agent doesn't say why either, but it leaves something a person never does: a complete record of every request it made and every answer it got. Pick a way for checkout to go wrong, and a real agent will shop a real (fictional) store over HTTP while you watch all three sides.

QUESTION 1 · DIAGNOSIS

Why did it stop?

The evidence engine. It reads only what the shop's server saw, plus the shop's own catalogue, and tests 13 possible causes against it.

  • An explicit reason on the response, like 402 insufficient_funds, is trusted first
  • Then behaviour: where it stopped, measured against what it was served
  • What the agent says only counts if the traffic agrees
  • No evidence: answer is UNKNOWN, never a guess
a cause and a confidence
QUESTION 2 · THE 11 RULES

Should we do anything?

The policy gate. It doesn't work out why; it decides whether acting is allowed. It's a fixed checklist with no AI in it.

  • Is the diagnosis confident enough? (R-10)
  • Have we already tried too often, or given away too much margin? (R-01, R-02, R-04)
  • Is it worth more than it costs? Does it need a human's consent? (R-05, R-07, R-09)
  • Has an operator pulled the kill switch? (R-11)
