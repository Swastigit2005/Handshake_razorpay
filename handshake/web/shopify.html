Your Shopify store, open to AI agents

Shopify checkout ends on a page a person fills in, so an AI agent can't buy from it directly. Handshake runs an agent-facing checkout in front of your store, backed by your real products, Shopify's own price and tax calculation, and real (test) orders in your admin. Connect Claude to it, and Handshake explains every agent that stops, from the traffic alone.
